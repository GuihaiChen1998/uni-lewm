\caption{\textbf{Uni-LeWM overview.} A single Transformer and trainable readout serve observation encoding and action-conditioned latent prediction. Image tokens produce state and goal embeddings, while latent--action tokens produce autoregressive imagined states. At each control step, categorical CEM minimizes the predicted terminal distance to the encoded goal, executes the first action, and incorporates the next observation. Training combines multi-step prediction MSE with SIGReg on encoded states, with gradients through both prediction and target branches. Termination uses the distance from the current observed state to the goal. Modality-specific BatchNorm statistics support the shared readout.}
\label{fig:uni-lewm-overview}
